The problem is simple, and it still burns a lot of time. At close, a controller needs to know: can I run this yet? Can I execute this rec? Can I produce the 15C3? Today that answer is scattered — Motif, CATS, Helix, SAP, Axiom — each with its own screen and its own status word. Nobody holds the whole picture, so people chase. We fold those facts into one ready or blocked answer.

You start by configuring the outcome. You don't stand up a new app. You write the question, the feeds it depends on, how tight the SLA is, and what should happen when everything's in. This month-end close example is just journals and cost-centre sign-off. That's data. Save it, and it's live.

Then the owner comes here, to Configuration. Pick the outcome on the left. On the right is the actual contract — who can see it, the SLA, the action when it's ready, and every feed that has to complete. If a dependency is wrong, you catch it here, not after the close has slipped.

When you want to run that outcome for the day, you don't do it from the product pages. You go to Drive, pick the scenario for that business date, and start it. The board and the reports stay as the real job. They only show the fold.

If the day goes well — look at 15C3 — every feed arrives. It goes ready, then processing, then the report's generated, and then it's available to view. A controller can open it and actually proceed.

If it doesn't go well, you don't get a vague red flag. This rec is blocked because Motif rejected ledger MB014. That named key. The person doing the work can escalate it to run-the-bank, instead of opening another ticket and hoping.

For a CIO or an MD, this is the management screen. Outcome board. Traffic lights across the unit — what's ready, what's blocked, how many escalations are open. No book grid. No engine internals. And it works on a phone. Same lights, between meetings. A mobile app can wrap this later. The job doesn't change.

Run-the-bank is a different job. They watch delays, escalations, dead letters. They can replay something that failed. They don't sign off the rec. That's still the outcome user's call.
