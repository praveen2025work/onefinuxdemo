The problem is straightforward, and it still costs a lot of time and a lot of control. At close, leadership still cannot get a clean answer to a simple question. Can we run this process? Can we execute this rec? Can we produce the fifteen C three? Today that answer lives across Motif, CATS, Helix, SAP, Axiom. Each has its own screen, and its own language for done. So people chase. We fold those facts into one answer. Ready, or blocked, and why.

You start by configuring the outcome. This is not a new application. You define the question, the feeds it depends on, the SLA, and what should happen when everything is in. Month end close is journals and cost centre sign off. That is data. Once it is saved, it is live.

The owner then comes to Configuration. Select the outcome on the left. On the right is the contract. Who is entitled to see it, the SLA, the action when it is ready, and every feed that must complete. If a dependency is wrong, you see it here. Not after the close has already slipped.

When you want to run that outcome for the day, you do it from Drive, not from the product screens. You start it for that business date. The board and the reports stay as the operating view. They only show the fold.

When the day goes well, this is fifteen C three. Every feed arrives. It moves to ready, the report is generated, and it is available to view. The controller can open it and proceed, with a full trail.

When it does not, you do not get an unexplained red. This rec is blocked because Motif rejected ledger M B oh fourteen. A named key. The person on the outcome escalates it to run the bank. They are not opening another ticket and hoping.

For a CIO or an MD, this is the management screen. Outcome board. Traffic lights across the unit. What is ready, what is blocked, how many escalations are open. No book grid. No engine internals. And it works on a phone. Same board, between meetings. A mobile app can wrap this later. The job does not change.

Run the bank has a different mandate. Delays, escalations, dead letters. They can replay a failed event. They do not sign off the rec. That remains with the outcome owner.
